# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Benchhand a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@anshcantcode](https://github.com/anshcantcode), using the email address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Do not report conduct problems in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and take the action they judge appropriate, which can be one of the following:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning that explains why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban:** a ban from any interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code.
